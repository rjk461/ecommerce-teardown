About Richard

In 2009, I co-founded Beer Cartel out of a Kennards Storage Shed with a goal to build Australia's best online craft beer store. By the time we sold in 2024, it was Australia's #1 online craft beer retailer — high 7-figure revenue, 150,000+ customers, and a 4.8 Google rating maintained through the chaos of COVID.

As Head of Ecommerce and CMO (acting), I owned the full commercial operation — P&L, platform strategy, paid media, email, CRO, team leadership, and board reporting. Four direct reports (Marketing, Procurement, Operations, Finance) and quarterly board presentations to co-founders and an external finance expert.

Some of the numbers: $1M revenue in 4 days from the Beer Advent Calendar. 5:1 ROAS on a high six-figure annual paid media budget across Meta and Google. Email at 30% of total revenue through advanced segmentation and lifecycle automation. BigCommerce to Shopify migration in 90 days — zero downtime, $100K in savings. Elected board member of Retail Drinks Australia alongside C-level executives from Coles, Endeavour, and Metcash.

Now I deliver private teardowns of ecommerce sites, help brands fix what's costing them conversions, and work with AI tools — genuinely, not just for the LinkedIn post.
