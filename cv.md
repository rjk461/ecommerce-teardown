## From research to online retail

I am Richard Kelsey, a Sydney-based ecommerce and AI consultant and co-founder of Made 4 Tradies. Before online retail, I worked in market research. That background shaped how Geoff Huens and I built Beer Cartel and the Australian Craft Beer Survey.

Today I advise Australian online retailers on growth, customer acquisition and practical AI. At Made 4 Tradies, the focus is websites and marketing for trade businesses. [My Made 4 Tradies biography](https://made4tradies.com.au/about/richard-kelsey) explains that part of my work.

## Experience and current work

1.  **2009 to September 2025: Beer Cartel.** Co-founder and CEO, building the business from a storage shed into Australia's #1 online craft beer retailer. The business was sold in 2024; I stayed through the transition until September 2025.
2.  **2016 to 2022: Australian Craft Beer Survey.** Worked with Geoff, the Beer Cartel team and industry partners on six published research editions. [Read the survey case study](https://ecommerceteardown.com/case-studies/australian-craft-beer-survey).
3.  **2019 to September 2025: Retail Drinks Australia.** Board member representing online liquor retailers.
4.  **Current: Made 4 Tradies.** Co-founder, applying ecommerce and marketing experience to Australian trade businesses.
5.  **May 2026 to present: independent consulting.** Ecommerce growth strategy, customer acquisition planning and practical AI for marketing and operations.

## Recognition
